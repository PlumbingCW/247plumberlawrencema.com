Plumber in Mount Vernon, Lawrence, MA

Mount Vernon is a hill on the southern side of Lawrence with a mix of single-family houses and small multi-family buildings. This page is for owners who want to maintain and plan, not only fix breakdowns.

The Mount Vernon Neighborhood Association meets three times a year at the Frost School on Hamlet Street. The area is in the 01843 ZIP code, and the city's water department lists the Mount Vernon Tanks among the places it stores finished water.

Data sites built on census figures show more detached houses here than in Arlington or North Common, along with two-family and three- and four-unit buildings. That mix points to owners who manage their own heating, hot water and drains.

What we can say about Mount Vernon

A local geography source describes Mount Vernon as a hill along the southern edge of the city. Data sites list Mount Vernon Street, Chandler Road, Beacon Street, South Bowdoin Street and Greenwood Road in or near the area. We do not draw boundaries, because sources disagree on them.

We also do not claim any plumbing problem is common here. What we can do is lay out the jobs that every owner of a house like these will face sooner or later and the Massachusetts rules that apply to them.

Boilers and hot water: who may do what

Gas boiler piping, venting and connections are gas fitting work that requires a license and a gas permit. Oil burners are installed by technicians certified by the Massachusetts Department of Fire Services, and a plumbing license alone does not cover that work. Ask any contractor which certification applies to your system.

State boiler inspection rules exempt boilers in private residences, so a yearly service check is your decision. Rental owners have a firmer duty. The state sanitary code requires heat of at least 68 F from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. and 64 F overnight between September 15 and May 31 (105 CMR 410.180). See boiler repair for what a failed circulator or a lost pressure reading means.

For hot water, state plumbing code caps residential delivery at 130 F (248 CMR 10.14). A tank that is leaking or past its useful life points to water heater replacement, and a family that wants endless hot water should read about a tankless water heater first.

If you smell gas

Boilers and water heaters run on gas in many houses. Act first and diagnose later.

  • Leave the building right away and take everyone with you.
  • Do not use light switches, phones or anything that can spark while you are inside.
  • Call 911 from outside.
  • Then call the gas company. Mass.gov lists Eversource Gas at (800) 525-8222.
  • Do not go back in until the gas company says it is safe.

The sewer lateral on a hill lot

The lateral is the pipe that carries waste from your house to the city sewer main. In Lawrence the owner owns it, so a break or a root problem found after you buy is yours to fix. A sewer camera inspection before closing shows what is underground.

Gurgling, slow drains in more than one fixture and sewage smells in the basement can all point to the lateral. A single slow sink is usually a local blockage, and drain cleaning is the first step. Before installing a backwater valve, ask the sewer authority, as the state flooding and sewage back-up guide advises.
