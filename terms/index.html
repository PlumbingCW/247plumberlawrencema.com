Booking Service by Phone

Service starts with a call to our dispatch line, open 24/7. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

A call is a request, not a contract for a fixed time. We offer same-day appointments when available, and we do not promise arrival times, because traffic, weather and call volume vary.

Pricing and Payment

You receive a clear price before work starts. Work begins only after you approve it. If the plumber finds a different problem during the job, the plumber will explain it and give you a revised price before more work is done.

Payment terms are explained when the price is given. Permit fees, if any, are explained at that time.

Cancellations and Access to Your Property

If you need to cancel or move an appointment, call the dispatch line as early as you can so the schedule can be adjusted. Please tell us if the plan changes before the plumber reaches your address.

You agree that a responsible adult will be present or that you have arranged access to the property, including the water shutoff, the water heater and any area where the work is needed. If safe access is not possible, the plumber may need to reschedule.

Licensing, Permits and Access

Our plumbers are licensed and insured. You can verify any plumber in the state's eLIPSE system. Plumbing and gas fitting work that is replaced or repaired needs a permit from the local inspector, and you agree to give the access needed to complete permitted work.

If you rent, you confirm you have the owner's or landlord's permission for the work, or that the owner or landlord authorizes it.

Your Responsibilities

Please tell us about hazards you know of, such as a gas smell, standing water near electrical equipment, or damaged ceilings. Keep children and pets away from the work area.

If you ask for work that cannot be done safely or legally, the plumber may decline it and explain why.

Emergencies and Safety

If anyone is in danger, call 911. If you smell gas, leave the building, call 911 from outside and call your gas utility before calling us.

Our line is not a substitute for emergency services.
